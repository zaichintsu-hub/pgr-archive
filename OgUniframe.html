CONCEPT FILE

ORIGINAL VANGUARD MODEL FIX

MODERNIZED ROLE EXPANSION & COMPETITIVE REINTEGRATION

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This concept preserves the original purpose and direction of Uniframe Vanguards while establishing a modernized, fixed model that moves them away from a niche identity and strongly encourages regular player investment.

The original A-Rank-like investment approach is preserved, following the original investment system:

  • 100% guarantee at 10 pulls via Uniframe Banner.
  • No CUB requirement.
  • Duplicates grant 18 Inver Shards.

At the same time, the model expands their relevance into more endgame content, rather than making their competitive value dependent on niche Uniframe-specific stages.

Note: This model applies only to future Uniframes. The current seven Uniframe Vanguards retain the existing model and receive no updates under this concept.

FIXED VANGUARD MODEL SPECIFICATIONS

UNIFRAME SKILLS

At SSS Rank, the character unlocks additional functionality for their Uniframe Skills, allowing Speed Attack and Finishing Move to interact with regular enemies that lack a Finisher Gauge:

UNIFRAME SKILLS

SPEED ATTACK

In addition to the original kit: A Perfect Parry triggers a 1s Quick Time Event (indicated by a flashing Dodge Button), during which holding Dodge will deplete 30% Dodge Gauge to trigger Speed Attack.

FINISHING MOVE

In addition to the baseline functionality, I suggest giving it a special trigger condition that triggers only against regular enemies and does not trigger against enemies with Finisher Gauge, fitting into the character’s kit and rotation + an execution threshold.

Example: After triggering Assault-Rising Combo on a target with less than 30% remaining HP, tapping Basic Attack will have Noan: Arca trigger Finishing Move. Note: This skill will not be implemented for Noan: Arca under this concept, as the existing seven Uniframes retain the legacy approach. Noan: Arca is used here solely as an example to illustrate the trigger.

Boss-Type Enemies: For regular bosses lacking the Finisher Gauge, whether to make this new trigger condition effective against them or not is up to Kuro.

CHARACTER SKILLS, CORE SKILLS AND KIT

Character Skills, Core Skills, and overall kits will focus more heavily on Burst DMG and Single-Target DMG, with less emphasis on AoE and DoT. This gives Uniframes greater potential in single-target and speedrun stages than in multi-target or wave-based combat.

EFFECT SYSTEM: Similarly to modern characters, Uniframes unlock the Effect and its mechanics via Core Skills. Incorporates a unique consumption mechanism that supports the burst and speedrun specialty:

  • Triggering [a character-specific skill fitting into their rotation, such as Signature Move] instantly consumes all accumulated Effect stacks to deal Effect Burst DMG based on the Effect's current charge level.
// EXTRA OPTIONAL SUGGESTIONS

NEW EFFECT

Introducing a new Effect with its team composition being:

  • The consumer (DPS) is always a Vanguard.
  • The charger and buffer are the Omniframe damage reduction and amplifying classes (Tank / Breaker / Amplifier / Overlinker).
  • No Attacker unit will have access to this effect.
  • Vanguard units will still have access to other effects.

The goal is to give Vanguard a guaranteed role, being the Effect’s main consumer at all times.

PHYSICAL-SECONDARY EFFECT: RUPTURE
  • Stacking: Maximum of 10 stacks. Every 2 stacks increase Rupture's level by 1.
  • Consumption: Instantly consumes all available Rupture stacks at once, depleting the target's Armor Bar and dealing Effect Burst DMG based on Rupture's level.

WEAPON HARMONY I

Slight System Changes: When a fully equipped 6-piece Uniframe Memory Set is used for Weapon Harmony, the Memory Set level will further increase.

META AND TEAM COMPOSITIONS

VANGUARD VS ATTACKER

  • An S-Rank Attacker outperforms an S-Rank Vanguard of equal rank.
  • A Vanguard outperforms an A-Rank Attacker of equal rank.
  • An SSS-Rank Vanguard outperforms SS- and SS3-Rank Attacker.

SINGLE-TARGET COMBAT

  • A Vanguard outperforms an S-Rank Attacker of equal rank (up to SSS-Rank).
  • The performance level of an SSS-Rank Vanguard and an SSS-Rank Attacker is close.
  • An SSS+ Rank Vanguard can be weaker than an SSS+ Rank Attacker.

RESULTS

  • Attacker remains the stronger general-purpose DPS class.
  • Vanguard becomes the second best-in-slot after Attacker, rather than a niche specialist.
  • Vanguard offers the more accessible investment path for F2P and mid-spending players.
  • Attacker remains the stronger option for high investment and maximum general combat performance.
  • In single-target and time-sensitive stages such as Phantom Pain Cage, Vanguard becomes the preferred option.
  • Finisher Gauge stages remain Vanguard's strongest native environment, but Vanguard is no longer dependent on them for its competitive value.
