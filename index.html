PHILOSOPHY

  • Uniframes have to get more options through which their exclusive mechanics can shine (e.g. Game modes / New trigger conditions).
  • Uniframes have to be relevant to regular endgame content, or else why would a player invest in a niche unit?
  • Uniframes power-creeping or replacing Attackers should be avoided, by granting them extra specialties, giving them more advantage under certain conditions, and less advantage at other conditions.
  • Uniframes' investment model should depart from the A-Rank-like model and adapt to the S-Rank model, or else Uniframes will technically become 6 times cheaper Attackers.
  • Developers should have a valid reason to release new Uniframe units, through profitability and minimized development effort.
  • Any rework or structural code changes must be entirely avoided/overly minimized.
  • The A-rank-like approach should be abandoned, sticking to it makes Uniframes = A-Rank units with extra mechanics, development work, and stage requirements. Additionally, it is the main reason why giving them a niche role is the only possible way to make players invest in non-competitive units. If necessary, this approach can be followed (NOT APPLIED): [A-Rank = 4 star / S-Rank Uniframe = 5 star / S-Rank Omniframe = 6 star], by applying necessary balance and investment requirements.

  • I highly recommend applying my concepts to a new Uniframe Class while simply abandoning Vanguard, which, I believe, opens a window to many possibilities and saves more effort and balancing issues. In any case, all of my concepts support both New Class Introduction & Retaining Vanguard approaches at the same time.
