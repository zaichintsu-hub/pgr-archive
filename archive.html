CONCEPT 01

CORROSION AFFIX

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

Rather than a full class rework, this concept suggests reviving Uniframes (Vanguard Class) by linking Uniframe skills to Affixes.

By using two already-existing mechanics, this becomes a cost-friendly rework; it’s factual that we will be introduced to more elemental Secondary-Affixes in the future (we got Lighting–Raydiance and Nihil–Disruption already), as for Uniframe skills, the way they function will remain unchanged, they will simply be given new trigger conditions based on the Affix.

The Physical Secondary-Affix will be Uniframe-exclusive. Can be unlocked via Core skills for future Uniframes.

MAIN IDEA: CORROSION AFFIX

Corrosion is the new Physical-Secondary Affix, exclusive to future Uniframe units and is inaccessible to Omniframe units.

In-Game Description: “Generates highly reactive entropic particles to systematically erode the target’s structural integrity. When the accumulated energy reaches a critical state, it overrides the target’s defensive structure, allowing the frame to convert its attacks into powerful corroding damage and exploit the resulting instability.”

CORROSION AFFIX MECHANICS

  • Stacking: Builds up to a maximum of 20 stacks.
  • Activation: Overrides the target’s Armor Gauge, converting it into Finisher Gauge.
  • Damage Output: Corrosion DMG scales with the number of accumulated stacks. Deals primarily Affix DMG, and secondarily Elemental DMG.
  • Restriction: Finishing Move does not trigger against regular Boss-type enemies who originally do not have Finisher Gauge.

STACK CONSUMPTION

  • Holding Dodge while dodging incoming attacks consumes 3 stacks to trigger Speed Attack. Can trigger even when Corrosion is inactive, as long as the current stacks ≥ 3.
  • When the target’s Armor Gauge has been fully depleted, consumes 5 stacks to trigger Finishing Move. Only triggers when Corrosion is inactive.

DEACTIVATION

  • Stacks < 5: Cannot trigger Finishing Move.
  • Stacks < 3: Effect deactivates.

TEAM COMPOSITIONS & ENDGAME CONTENT

TEAM COMPOSITIONS

  • ELEMENTAL TEAM: Vanguard will compete with Attacker for the main DPS slot, alongside Breaker/Tank and Amplifier/Overlinker.
  • CORROSION TEAM: At least 2 Uniframe units are required to achieve maximum DMG output. A Physical Amplifier can help increase the DMG output, since Corrosion is a Physical Affix. Uniframe’s Enhanced Team skill serves as the second buff provider for the team.

ENDGAME CONTENT

  • WARZONE (Corrosion Modifier): “Enemies’ HP stats increases by 35% every wave. All members gain +5% Corrosion DMG every time they trigger Finishing Move”. The stage features tanky enemies that can be defeated by depleting their Armor Gauge and then executing them.
  • OPERATION UNIFRAME: This mode could get little modifications and return as a main game mode with weekly tasks.

UNIFRAME PATCH NOTES

UPDATE UNIFRAME GACHA BANNER

  • Introduce exclusive R&D tickets, earned through Operation Uniframe.
  • Increase the pity threshold to 60.
  • A duplicate Uniframe pull grants 30 Inver Shards.

FUTURE UNIFRAME UNITS

  • Apply Time Lag Calculation to Signature Moves and Finishing Moves.
  • Grant them Corrosion DMG buffs via their 6-piece memory sets.
  • Grant them CUBs.

INVESTMENT

  • Increase the availability of Uniframe investment materials through the Guild Shop and Operation Uniframe rewards.
CONCEPT 02

RAIDER CLASS

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This concept revolves around “How Uniframes can regain their competitiveness, without making any modifications to Vanguard class or already-existing mechanics?”

My proposal is to introduce a new class that enables Uniframes and supports Vanguards.

Main Idea: Raider Class

In-Game Description: “Focused on disruption, can manipulate enemy behavior and override their defenses while strengthening allies.”

Raider is a new Uniframe class, whose roles are divided into a general role and a Uniframe specialization.

CLASS MECHANICS & SKILLS

CLASS SKILL (MAX LEVEL)

Raider QTEs grants 1 Shatter that increases the Extra DMG Bonus by 5% and the Raider's Elemental DMG Bonus by 5% to self and allies, up to a maximum of 4 stacks. When Raider enters the battlefield, grants 1 Shatter. On exit, consumes all available Shatter to deal 500% / 1000% / 1500% / 2000% Elemental DMG of the Raider element.

UNIFRAME SPECIALIZATION

QTEs from a Raider unit mark the target nearest to the QTE Area of Effect as [Executable]. Does not apply to Boss-type enemies.
[Executable]: Finishing Move can trigger against an Executable target.
Note: Applies to only one enemy per QTE trigger.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

Raider units retain standard Uniframe Skill mechanics, with specific enhancements applied to:

  • Enhanced Speed Attack: In addition to dealing elemental Burst DMG, marks the target who performed the All-out Attack as [Executable].
  • Enhanced Team: When any Uniframe in the team uses Finishing Move, grants the maximum number of Shatter stacks and increase the Radier Skill effect by 50% for 15s.

ANALYSIS: TEAM COMPOSITIONS

ELEMENTAL & EFFECT COMPOSITION

Team Layout: [ATTACKER/VANGUARD - BREAKER/TANK - AMPLIFIER/RAIDER]

DPS Comparison:

  • Vanguard technically functions like an Attacker for 10 seconds upon deployment.
  • Uniframe 6-piece memory sets provide higher DMG stats than Omniframe 4-piece memory sets.
  • Omniframe has the advantage of being able to equip 2 complete memory sets thanks to Weapon Harmony.
  • Some Omniframe memory sets like Cottie provide the advantage of a faster rotation.

SUPPORT Comparison:

  • Amplifier instantly provides 20% Extra DMG Bonus for 10 seconds when a character is deployed, while Raider builds up stacks through QTEs, with the maximum Extra DMG Bonus being 20%.
  • Amplifier heals allies, increases their DMG, and provides more buffs via support Omniframe memory sets like Da Vinci and Guinevere.
  • Raider deals AoE Burst DMG on exit.
  • [Executable] opens a window for Uniframes to partly benefit from Uniframe skills’ Finishing Move and Enhanced Team’s stats buff.

Conclusion: Vanguard and Attacker, Raider and Amplifier, can share the same slot, depending on which unit is stronger.

UNIFRAME COMPOSITION

Team Layout: [VANGUARD - VANGUARD - RAIDER]
  • Outside of dedicated Uniframe stages, this composition is less effective than standard competitive Omniframe teams.
  • Gains stats buff and support from Raider's Class skill and Uniframes’ Enhanced Team.
  • Specializes against enemies with Finisher Gauge and enemies whose HP and shield stats are high.
  • Omniframe units are highly irrelevant to this team.

UNIFRAME PATCH NOTES

COMBAT STAGES

  • Add more Uniframe end-game content, Operation Uniframe may return as a main Uniframe game mode.
  • Uniframe combat stages feature enemies with high HP and Finisher Gauge stats.

UNIFRAME GACHA BANNER

  • Release a new Uniframe Raider.
  • Introduce exclusive Uniframe Banner R&D Tickets earned through Operation Uniframe.
  • Increase the pity threshold to 60.
  • A duplicate Uniframe copy grants 30.

FUTURE UNIFRAME UNITS

  • Apply Time Lag Calculation to Signature Moves and Finishing Moves.
  • Grant them CUBs.

INVESTMENT

  • Increase the availability of Uniframe investment materials through the Guild Shop and Operation Uniframe rewards.
CONCEPT 03

NEW UNIFRAME MEMORY SET CATEGORY

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

I call it the Capsule Concept; it suggests reviving Uniframes simply by introducing a single new category of Memory Sets. This concept introduces a new category of Uniframe Memory Set in addition to their standard 6-piece sets.

SPECIFICATIONS

  • A complete set consists of 2 pieces.
  • Exclusive to Uniframes, with a single universal set and character-specialized sets (for future Uniframe units).
  • The Memory Set skill enables Uniframe Skills’ mechanics outside of dedicated Uniframe stages.

FUNCTIONALITY

UNIVERSAL SET

Triggering a 3-orb ping grants [Shatter]. The next attack consumes [Shatter] to deal 500% Elemental AoE DMG, and apply 1 stack of [Executable] on up to 3 targets. When a target is inflicted with 3 [Executable] stacks, Finishing Move can trigger against this target, with a 5 seconds countdown.

CHARACTER-SPECIALIZED SET

It functions the same as the universal set, with the only difference being the trigger condition for granting [Shatter], which adapts directly to the character’s unique kit.

NOTES

  • Restriction: Equipping one 2-piece Uniframe Memory Set locks out others; a Uniframe cannot equip multiple sets of this category simultaneously.
  • Finisher Gauge enemies: Cannot be excuted via [Executable] in the presence of Finisher Gauge.
  • Finishing Moves: Unusable against standard Boss-type enemies (excluding Uniframe Bosses like MC Solar).
  • [Executable]: Opens a window for Uniframes to utilize Uniframe Skills against normal and elite enemies lacking the Finisher Gauge.
  • Skill Inheritance: If an enemy dies without being executed, another enemy inherits [Executable]. If the enemy is executed or the 5-seconds countdown expires, [Executable] expires.
Mechanic Advantage Note: This concept makes Weapon Harmony an essential build attribute for Uniframes, as a player can now use a 6-piece memory set, and fill the 2-piece memory slot provided by Weapon Harmony with the new memory set category, making it preferable to pull 6★ Signature weapons.
CONCEPT 04

MODERN UNIFRAME APPROACH

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This concept preserves the original identity of Uniframes as Burst and Execution units. It builds upon the developers' original direction by introducing a streamlined approach for future Uniframe characters. This approach applies to future Uniframe units only.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

SPEED ATTACK

In addition to the original skill: A Perfect Parry triggers a 1s Quick Time Event (indicated by a flashing Dodge button), during which holding Dodge consumes 50% dodge gauge to trigger Speed Attack.

Recommendation: Apply Time Lag Calculation to Speed Attack.

FINISHING MOVE

In addition to the baseline functionality, I suggest giving it a special trigger condition that triggers only against regular enemies and does not trigger against enemies with Finisher Gauge, fitting into the character’s kit and rotation + an execution threshold.

Apply Time Lag Calculation to Finishing Move.

Enhanced Speed Attack and Enhanced Finishing Move retain their original functionality.

ENHANCED TEAM

After any Uniframe in the team uses Finishing Move, Increases [the controlled character’s element] DMG of all Uniframes in the team by 10% for 15s, and either:

  • Refreshes Vanguard Class Skill duration and increase its effect by 50%.
  • Restores 20% of max HP to self and allies and increase the next Uniframe’s ATK by 10% for 15s.
  • Reduces Finisher Gauge effectiveness by 20% for all enemies within range for 15s.

A new Uniframe unit simply gets one of these skills. The goal is to create multi-role slots in the Uniframe Team Comps.

NEW SKILL PROGRESSION

CORE SKILL (CHARACTER-SPECIFIC)

Unlocks the character’s effect and its mechanics by default.
Vanguard units possess a specialized method of interacting with effects that directly aligns with their Burst role.

  • Vanguards are slow at charging the effect.
  • Triggering a Signature Move while the effect is active consumes all accumulated stacks to deal Effect Burst DMG, scaling proportionally with the effect's current charge level.

6★ SIGNATURE WEAPON

For future Uniframes, 6★ Signature Weapons will get special skills via Harmony Awakening, making it more preferable for players to pull for them.

Suggestion: If the 6-Piece Memory set is already fully equipped, using the same set to harmonize will further increase the Memory set level.

NOTES AND ANALYSIS

  • Finishing Move: Can’t be triggered against a standard Boss-type enemy lacking the Finisher Gauge.
  • New Enhanced Team: Defines the specializations within a Uniframe team composition. The base skill, combined with one of three varying role skills, establishes three distinct specializations: Primary DPS, Sustainer and Disrupter.
  • Effect Burst: Combines the original Burst identity of Uniframes with effects, introducing a new unique role for effect team composition:
    • Burst: Uses Vanguard as the main effect consumer to instantly deal effect burst DMG and complete the stage faster. Recommended for PPC.
    • DoT: Uses the main Omniframe DPS to deal effect DMG over time, being the best at dealing with multi-wave stages within the stage time. Recommended for WZ.
  • Perfect Parry: Triggered by parrying an enemy at a perfect moment in time. Can be triggered against all types of enemies.
  • Original Speed Attack & Finishing Move: Just pointing out that the original functionality is included within the new approach; Speed Attacks are triggered when dodging the target's All-out Attack by holding Dodge, Finishing Moves are triggered when the target has an empty Finisher Gauge. The new approach is simply added to the original functionality.
