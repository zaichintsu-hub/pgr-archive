IDENTITY BASED CONCEPTS

TERMINATOR CLASS

CONCEPT 01

TERMINATOR CLASS

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This concept introduces a new Uniframe class featuring a Class Skill and core kit designed to expand and sharpen the identity of Uniframe Skills.

CLASS SPECIFICATIONS

Just as Uniframe Skills emphasize burst output and Max HP-scaling damage, the Terminator's Core skills, Evolution skills, and Class Skill are built entirely around these two damage types. Additionally, essential skills like the Signature Move will feature Time Lag Calculation (TLC).

In-game Description: Focuses on overcoming, can deal sever damage to enemies with high durability.

CLASS SKILL

Since I do not have access to the exact enemy HP values used in development, the scaling below is provided as a conceptual baseline:
Every 2× increase in the target's Max HP relative to the character's Max HP grants +5% Extra DMG Bonus, up to +40%.

Demonstration: When an enemy has Max HP equal to 100% × 2n of the character’s Max HP, the character gains a +5% Extra DMG Bonus proportional to the value of n, capping at 40%. Mathematically, it follows:

  • Enemy Max HP = 100% × 21 = 200% Character Max HP → +5% Extra DMG Bonus
  • 100% × 22 = 400% → +10%
  • 100% × 23 = 800% → +15%
  • 100% × 24 = 1,600% → +20%
  • 100% × 25 = 3,200% → +25%
  • 100% × 26 = 6,400% → +30%
  • 100% × 27 = 12,800% → +35%
  • 100% × 28 = 25,600% → +40% (MAX)
Note: When facing multiple enemies, the Class Skill scales based on the target with the highest Max HP.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

These mechanics are designed to support the character in multi-target mob encounters where enemy Max HP is relatively low, compensating for the resulting drop in the Class Skill's Extra DMG Bonus.

SKILL ADJUSTMENTS

SPEED ATTACK

Upon triggering Matrix, holding Dodge will trigger Speed Attack and deplete a percentage of the Dodge Gauge.
This shift makes Speed Attacks a proactive part of the character's core rotation rather than a purely reactive tool.

FINISHING MOVE

The trigger condition will be based on a Uniframe mechanic: the [Executable] status.

[Executable]: Finishing Move can trigger against an Executable target under certain conditions.

Two suggested trigger methods for this mechanic:

  • Recommended: When 4 or more enemies are present, applies [Executable] to up to 4 targets, with a X seconds cooldown. When an [Executable] target falls below 30% HP, the character triggers Finishing Move.
  • Defeating enemies grants stacks that expire after 10s. Accumulating 5 stacks enables [Executable] for X seconds.

Applying Time Lag Calculation to Finishing Move should be considered and evaluated by the developers.

Extra Suggestion: If TLC will not apply to Finishing Move, players must have the freedom to manually trigger the move to prevent rotation disruption. Suggested manual inputs include:

  • Basic Attack.
  • Hold Dodge, then tap Basic Attack.
  • Hold the CUB button.

Toggleable through Finishing Move skill settings.

ENHANCED TEAM

Increases the Terminator Class Skill effect.

ENHANCED SPEED ATTACK & ENHANCED FINISHING MOVE

Retain their original kit design.

With these adjustments, the Finisher Gauge is no longer the sole requirement to activate Uniframe Skills. Enemies with a Finisher Gauge effectively become the Uniframe's secondary specialization.

Note: The baseline mechanics for Speed Attacks, Finishing Moves, and the Finisher Gauge depletion remain intact; these new conditions simply act as additional triggers.

AFFIX IDENTITY

There are 2 directions concerning this mechanic:

  • Recommended: Affix Burst: Uses instant burst for stack consumption, rather than DoT – Triggering [a character-specific skill fitting into their rotation, such as Signature Move] instantly consumes all accumulated Affix stacks to deal Affix Burst DMG based on the Affix’s current charge level.
  • Exclusive Affix: Utilize one of the four empty Secondary-Affix slots to introduce a new Affix exclusive to Uniframes and completely inaccessible to Omniframes.

GAME MODES & BANNER SPECIFICATIONS

UNIFRAME BANNER REDESIGN

  • 100% guarantee for a Vanguard at 10 pulls.
  • 100% guarantee for a Terminator at 40 pulls (the exact pity value is up to Kuro).
  • Introduce exclusive Uniframe Banner R&D Tickets.
  • Terminator duplicates grant 30 Inver Shards.
  • Terminators do not gain a dedicated CUB.

GAME MODES

  • Bring back Operation Uniframe with minor adjustments, focusing on enemies with Finisher Gauges and offering exclusive Uniframe Banner R&D Tickets as a part of the weekly rewards.
  • Introduce a new game mode featuring enemies and bosses with massive HP pools, in addition to varied difficulty tiers, ranking, tasks and rewards. Whether this mode is weekly or seasonal is up to Kuro.
CONCEPT 02

UNIFRAME EXCLUSIVE ELEMENT

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

Future Uniframes will receive a new exclusive element inaccessible to Omniframe units. This element will serve as the restriction on Uniframe’s combat performance in regular stages, especially restricting Finishing Move mass-trigger.

All types of standard enemies except for Boss-type enemies will receive the Finisher Gauge as a default enemy mechanic. It will appear directly below the Armor Gauge, and by default will have a high capacity, provide no defensive properties, and an enemy will become executable once its Finisher Gauge is depleted.
Note that certain bosses (the Boss of the Uniframe character's story chapter) will have Finisher Gauge, with necessary modifiers.

UNIFRAME NEXT-GENERATION SPECIFICATIONS

ELEMENT

New Uniframe units will receive an exclusive secondary element, called UniElement under this concept.
Suggested Elements: Aether / Anemo / Corrosion / Hazard / Mist / Rift / Shatter / Toxin

Uniframes' energy output will be split between the main element and UniElement at a ratio of 70% Main Element + 30% UniElement. The developers will determine the exact values.

This split is intended to keep a new Uniframe's overall performance, including its Uniframe mechanics, slightly below that of a comparable Omniframe.

AFFIX

Since UniElement is a secondary damage type, it will not require its own exclusive elemental Affixes. However, specific endgame modifiers will be introduced to ensure that this element remains relevant.

As for how Uniframes will interact with their Affixes, I have 2 suggestions:

  • Affix Burst Behavior: The character's Core Passive will introduce an exclusive interaction with its assigned Affix, allowing the character to consume accumulated Affix resources to instantly deal Affix Burst DMG. The exact implementation will depend on the selected Affix and its existing mechanics. This behavior is especially ideal for Uniframes, as their combat stages usually feature single-target combat with massive HP and Finisher Gauge enemy stats, requiring fast depletion.
  • New Exclusive Affix: Utilize one of the available Affix slots (Primary-Affix [Nihil] // Secondary-Affix [Fire, Ice, Physical, Dark]) to introduce a new Affix exclusive for Uniframes and inaccessible to Omniframes, which will guarantee an additional active identity for Uniframes. Under this approach, Uniframes will have no access to regular Affixes.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

SKILL ADJUSTMENTS

SPEED ATTACK

I suggest giving it a new trigger condition besides the original one:
Upon triggering Matrix, the character can perform 1 Speed Attack (indicated by a flashing Dodge button).

FINISHING MOVE

Applying Time Lag Calculation (TLC) to Finishing Move should be considered and evaluated by the developers.

Extra Suggestion: If TLC will not apply to Finishing Move, players must have the freedom to manually trigger it to prevent rotation disruption.
Suggested manual inputs include:

  • Basic Attack.
  • Hold Dodge then tab Basic Attack.
  • Hold the CUB button.

ENDGAME CONTENT

As mentioned above, all enemies except for standard Bosses will receive the Finisher Gauge as a secondary gauge just below the Armor Gauge as a default enemy mechanic.

ELEMENTAL & AFFIX STAGES

Uniframes will be viable, beginner friendly/F2P DPS options in existing Elemental and Affix stages, with the intended balance target of performing below the leading competitive Omniframe in the relevant stage.

UNIELEMENT

Introduce a new stage modifier for UniElement. Since it accounts for only 30% of a new Uniframe's damage output, its specific modifiers will require separate tuning and may have higher effectiveness than regular elemental modifiers.

UniElement modifiers could also synergize with Uniframes’ exclusive mechanics, giving Uniframes an additional advantage in their dedicated content, and preventing the introduction of a new frustrating category of Uniframe team compositions for each main element, keeping the Uniframe team composition a single team without focus on the units’ main element.

  • Relevant Content: Warzone & Phantom Pain Cage (if necessary, PPC could feature a Uniframe Boss with a Finisher Gauge.)

UNIFRAME MECHANICS

    Introduce a new Uniframe game mode or bring back Operation Uniframe with any necessary modifications.

  • Rewards: Will offer a new type of R&D Tickets exclusive to the Uniframe Gacha Banner. This will distinguish Uniframe gacha from Omniframe gacha and reduce confusion over which tickets to use.

LEGACY UNIFRAMES

Under the implementation of this concept, the seven existing Uniframes will gain additional execution opportunities through the global Finisher Gauge change, and their combat performance will not be restricted by the new exclusive element. However, due to their outdated kits, lack of TLC, and the power creep accumulated over approximately five years, I believe that these additional opportunities are not expected to make them outperform modern competitive Omniframes. Thus, it is safe to say that if this approach had been applied during the active Uniframe era, it would have been risky, but in the current modern era, it wouldn't be.

CONCEPT 03

SINGLE-TARGET TEAM COMPOSITION

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

This concept updates the game’s combat meta by introducing a second team archetype: the Single-Target Team Composition, expanding the existing combat ecosystem by establishing distinct team specializations for multi-target combat and single-target combat, while bringing Punishing: Gray Raven’s meta structure into alignment with modern gacha gaming standards.

Uniframes serve as the core target of this concept, being the optimal class to specialize in single-target combat. Their kits will be designed to provide significantly stronger single-target performance than comparable Omniframes, while they will have limited multiple-targets and AoE capabilities, preventing them from replacing Omniframes in multi-target content.

Note: This concept does not propose redefining Omniframes as “Multiple-Target Specialists”, Omniframes retain their original, varied functionality, while only updating Uniframes to noticeably be better single-target specialists through character skills and kits.

MODERN UNIFRAME MODEL

OVERVIEW & APPROACH

The Single-Target DPS role will be established via one of two development approaches:

  • Approach A - Vanguard Modernization & Paradigm Shift: A modern generation of Vanguard units, retaining the original Class Skill. Can be distinguished from the older generation by slight UI changes.
    e.g. A different Uniframe mark on the character icon - different color / a star symbol attached in the center of the mark.
  • Approach B - New Uniframe Class: The introduction of a new Uniframe class category designed specifically to apply the new modernized model, while completely abandoning Vanguard class.

The new Uniframe model departs from the legacy A-Rank-style Uniframe investment model and adopts standard S-Rank parameters (60 pull guarantee, 30 Inver Shards on duplicate, dedicated CUBs, Harmony Awakening on Signature Weapons, Effect system, Time Lag Calculation).

MODERNIZED UNIFRAME BANNER

  • 100% guarantee at 60 pulls.
  • Duplicates grant 30 Inver Shards.
  • New exclusive Uniframe Banner Tickets, earned through Uniframe game mode – returned Operation Uniframe.
  • 100% guarantee for the original seven Uniframe Vanguards at 10 pulls + Their duplicates grant 18 Inver Shards.

The original Uniframe units (Camu: Crocotta, Qu: Pavo, Selena: Tempest, Roland: Flamebeau, Pulao: Dragontoll, Haicma: Starveil, Noan: Arca) will retain the older approach and will not be modernized.

CHARACTER SKILLS AND KIT

Will be repositioned and engineered to highly support the single-target specialty, focusing mainly on single-target DMG, Burst DMG, with less focus (or none) on AoE DMG, DoT DMG and Gather.
e.g. SSS Evolution Skill: Increases the Extra DMG Bonus by 50%, and by an additional 75% when facing less than 2 enemies.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

  • Redefined to slightly follow Leap Skills’ functionality.
  • From: Niche skills that specialize specifically against enemies with Finisher Gauge in Special Dispatch Areas.
  • To: A secondary specialization, partly supporting Uniframes in regular combat, and significantly enhancing their effectiveness in Special Dispatch Areas, where enemies have Finisher Gauge.
UNIFRAME SKILLS

SPEED ATTACK

Given another trigger condition in addition to its baseline functionality.
Suggestion: A Perfect Parry triggers a Quick Time Event (indicated by a flashing Dodge button), during which holding Dodge consumes 30% Dodge Gauge to trigger Speed attack.

FINISHING MOVE

In addition to the baseline functionality, I suggest giving it a special trigger condition that triggers only against regular enemies and does not trigger against enemies with Finisher Gauge, fitting into the character’s kit and rotation + an execution threshold.
e.g. After triggering Assault-Rising Combo on a target with less than 30% remaining HP, tapping Basic Attack will have Noan: Arca trigger Finishing Move. Note: This skill will not be implemented for Noan: Arca under this concept, as the existing seven Uniframes retain the legacy approach. Noan: Arca is used here solely as an example to illustrate the trigger.

Boss-Type Enemies: For standard bosses lacking the Finisher Gauge, whether to make this new trigger condition effective against them or not is up to Kuro.
In general, making Finishing Moves active in regular combat is totally up to Kuro to determine.

ENHANCED SPEED ATTACK & FINISHING MOVE

In addition to their baseline functionality, all Elemental DMG dealt by Speed Attack and Finishing Move is converted to Effect DMG when the character's Effect is active. These skills will serve as the most effective source of Burst DMG and AoE for higher efficiency in Uniframe Special Dispatch Areas, where there are multiple enemies with Finisher Gauge per stage.

ENHANCED TEAM

Make sure the original 2 skills are applied to the new Uniframes (one per character) in a regular manner, for varied roles in the Uniframe team composition.

  • After any Uniframe in the team finishes an enemy, restores 20% of self max HP and increases [the deployed character’s element & effect] DMG of all Uniframes in the team by 10% for 15s.
  • After any Uniframe in the team uses a Finishing Move, the Vanguard Skill of the next entering Uniframe increases by 50%, and its duration extends by 15s.

Suggestion: For the second skill, make sure it universally increases the next Uniframe's Class Skill effectiveness, in case of introducing new Uniframe classes later.

EFFECT SYSTEM

Similarly to modern characters, Uniframes unlock the Effect and its mechanics via Core Skills. Incorporates a unique consumption mechanism that supports the burst and speedrun specialty:

  • Triggering [a character-specific skill fitting into their rotation, such as Signature Move] instantly consumes all accumulated Effect stacks to deal Effect Burst DMG based on the Effect's current charge level.

HARMONY AWAKENING

Unlocks skills that support the identity and role of Uniframes via Signature Weapon’s Harmony Awakening skills.
e.g. When a normal or elite target has ≤ 25% HP, the next attack will instantly deplete the remaining HP. 45s cooldown.

WEAPON HARMONY I

Optional Suggestion: When a fully equipped 6-piece Uniframe Memory Set is used for Weapon Harmony, the Memory Set level will further increase.

ENDGAME CONTENT & TEAM COMPOSITIONS (ANALYSIS)

SINGLE-TARGET TEAM COMPS

  • Uses Vanguard as the main DPS.
  • Uses Breaker/Tank for DMG reduction, and Overlinker/Amplifier for team buffs and healing.
  • Best for single-target and Boss stages (e.g. Phantom Pain Cage).
  • Recommended: Introduce a new Uniframe class to occupy the Sub-DPS slot in later patches.

UNIFRAME TEAM COMPS (Special Dispatch Areas)

  • Uniframe’s second specialty team.
  • Uses 3 Uniframes / 2 Uniframes and one Omniframe for DMG amplification on standby.
  • Best for stages where enemies have Finisher Gauge.
  • Introducing a new Sub-DPS Uniframe class is also recommended for this composition.

STRIKER

Focused on execution, can exploit enemy vulnerabilities and unleash powerful strikes.

Gains 20% Extra DMG Bonus for 10s upon entering the battlefield. A 3-Ping grants Matrix Strike, up to a maximum of 3. When the character triggers Matrix, Matrix Strike will grant the next attack 20% / 30% / 40% Extra DMG Bonus. Stacks are lost upon character switch.

ECHOER

Focused on accumulation, can compound inflicted damage and release it in a powerful detonation.

Attacks apply Detonation Mark that accumulates 30% of all damage dealt to the target for 5s and then deals all accumulated damage.

WITHERER

Focused on erosion, can directly compromise and diminish the structural limits of enemies.

Attacks reduce the target's Max HP by 20% for 5s.

MARTYR

Focused on sacrifice, can convert frame damage into heightened offensive output while healing team members.

For every 2% HP lost, gain +0.6% Extra DMG Bonus.

CATALYST

Focused on synergy, can strengthen allies and unleash massive energy upon retreat.

Catalyst’s QTEs grant Catalysis that increases team members’ ATK by 7.5% and the Elemental DMG Bonus of the Catalyst's element by 7.5%, stacks up to a maximum of 4 stacks. When the Catalyst is switched in, gain 1 Catalysis. On exit, consumes all available Catalysis to deal 500% / 1000% / 1500% / 2000% Elemental DMG of the Catalyst's element.
