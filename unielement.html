SYSTEM: ONLINE
CONCEPT FILE

UNIFRAME EXCLUSIVE ELEMENT

NEW ELEMENT & MECHANICS OVERVIEW

CONCEPT OVERVIEW

Future Uniframes will receive a new exclusive element inaccessible to Omniframe units. This element will serve as the restriction on Uniframe’s combat performance in regular stages, especially restricting Finishing Move mass-trigger.

All types of standard enemies except for Boss-type enemies will receive the Finisher Gauge as a default enemy mechanic. It will appear directly below the Armor Gauge, and by default will have a high capacity, provide no defensive properties, and an enemy will become executable once its Finisher Gauge is depleted.
Note that certain bosses (the Boss of the Uniframe character's story chapter) will have Finisher Gauge, with necessary modifiers.

UNIFRAME NEXT-GENERATION SPECIFICATIONS

ELEMENT

New Uniframe units will receive an exclusive secondary element, called UniElement under this concept.
Suggested Elements: Aether / Anemo / Corrosion / Hazard / Mist / Rift / Shatter / Toxin

Uniframes' energy output will be split between the main element and UniElement at a ratio of 70% Main Element + 30% UniElement. The developers will determine the exact values.

This split is intended to keep a new Uniframe's overall performance, including its Uniframe mechanics, slightly below that of a comparable Omniframe.

AFFIX

Since UniElement is a secondary damage type, it will not require its own exclusive elemental Affixes. However, specific endgame modifiers will be introduced to ensure that this element remains relevant.

As for how Uniframes will interact with their Affixes, I have 2 suggestions:

  • Affix Burst Behavior: The character's Core Passive will introduce an exclusive interaction with its assigned Affix, allowing the character to consume accumulated Affix resources to instantly deal Affix Burst DMG. The exact implementation will depend on the selected Affix and its existing mechanics. This behavior is especially ideal for Uniframes, as their combat stages usually feature single-target combat with massive HP and Finisher Gauge enemy stats, requiring fast depletion.
  • New Exclusive Affix: Utilize one of the available Affix slots (Primary-Affix [Nihil] // Secondary-Affix [Fire, Ice, Physical, Dark]) to introduce a new Affix exclusive for Uniframes and inaccessible to Omniframes, which will guarantee an additional active identity for Uniframes. Under this approach, Uniframes will have no access to regular Affixes.

UNIFRAME SKILLS

SKILL ADJUSTMENTS

SPEED ATTACK

I suggest giving it a new trigger condition besides the original one:
Upon triggering Matrix, the character can perform 1 Speed Attack (indicated by a flashing Dodge button).

FINISHING MOVE

Applying Time Lag Calculation (TLC) to Finishing Move should be considered and evaluated by the developers.

Extra Suggestion: If TLC will not apply to Finishing Move, players must have the freedom to manually trigger it to prevent rotation disruption.
Suggested manual inputs include:

  • Basic Attack.
  • Hold Dodge then tab Basic Attack.
  • Hold the CUB button.

ENDGAME CONTENT

As mentioned above, all enemies except for standard Bosses will receive the Finisher Gauge as a secondary gauge just below the Armor Gauge as a default enemy mechanic.

ELEMENTAL & AFFIX STAGES

Uniframes will be viable, beginner friendly/F2P DPS options in existing Elemental and Affix stages, with the intended balance target of performing below the leading competitive Omniframe in the relevant stage.

UNIELEMENT

Introduce a new stage modifier for UniElement. Since it accounts for only 30% of a new Uniframe's damage output, its specific modifiers will require separate tuning and may have higher effectiveness than regular elemental modifiers.

UniElement modifiers could also synergize with Uniframes’ exclusive mechanics, giving Uniframes an additional advantage in their dedicated content, and preventing the introduction of a new frustrating category of Uniframe team compositions for each main element, keeping the Uniframe team composition a single team without focus on the units’ main element.

  • Relevant Content: Warzone & Phantom Pain Cage (if necessary, PPC could feature a Uniframe Boss with a Finisher Gauge.)

UNIFRAME MECHANICS

    Introduce a new Uniframe game mode or bring back Operation Uniframe with any necessary modifications.

  • Rewards: Will offer a new type of R&D Tickets exclusive to the Uniframe Gacha Banner. This will distinguish Uniframe gacha from Omniframe gacha and reduce confusion over which tickets to use.

LEGACY UNIFRAMES

Under the implementation of this concept, the seven existing Uniframes will gain additional execution opportunities through the global Finisher Gauge change, and their combat performance will not be restricted by the new exclusive element. However, due to their outdated kits, lack of TLC, and the power creep accumulated over approximately five years, I believe that these additional opportunities are not expected to make them outperform modern competitive Omniframes. Thus, it is safe to say that if this approach had been applied during the active Uniframe era, it would have been risky, but in the current modern era, it wouldn't be.
